| F8 | Publish and preserve the supported synthesis | Follow-up publication disposition, framework release and final evidence audit |

F0 and F1 begin in parallel. Individual experiments follow their existing source prerequisites; full W2 requires all W1 and E1–E4. Manuscript rebuilding follows the contribution decision. W7 remains protocol-only: F7/F8 need explicit new execution stages before acceptance. Negative, inconclusive or non-transferable outcomes remain valid research results, not automatic publication success.

F labels replace the earlier roadmap P0–P6 labels to avoid collision with existing publication gates P0–P4. See the completion plan for the migration crosswalk and precise completion definitions.
