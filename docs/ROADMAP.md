# Phased execution roadmap — 27 November 2026

This is a delivery view of the canonical [plan](PROJECT_PLAN.md), [state](PROJECT_STATE.json) and [task queue](work_packages/WORK_QUEUE.json), not a second scientific acceptance system. The immediate next phase ends with an accepted W1 evidence audit, reviewed E1–E4 results and a W4 contribution decision. A publishable manuscript is conditional on that decision.

No automatic tolerance widening. Source/reference bytes preserved unless a documented correction is accepted. New numerical results cannot be accepted as portable while their execution basis remains unresolved. |

## First work packets

1. P0: inspect `docs/validation/hosted-2026-11-24/` against a new local run; do not rerun blindly until green. Record max differences by unit and the exact environment, not one dimensionless aggregate.
2. In parallel, W1-01/02 and W1-03/04 can proceed from their specified literature. W1-05 and W1-06 are separate bounded evidence tasks. Obtain only the papers each task needs; do not reopen the unavailable Sahara or raw-survey requests.
3. After each relevant premise is accepted, implement and review its E1/E2/E4 packet. Assemble E3 after dependencies close. No blanket W2 completion until all required tasks and W1 are accepted.
4. Write the W3/W4 decision before committing to full manuscript rewriting or a broader simulation/astronomy programme.

## Reporting and input triggers

Report after P0 diagnosis, W1 acceptance, E1–E4 synthesis and W4 decision. Each update lists evidence accepted, unresolved limitations, changes from protocol and next executable task. Keep worker artifacts in one ignored directory and promote only independently reviewed outputs.

No further owner choice is needed to begin P0/W1. Raise a concrete question when essential unavailable access, paid resources, external contact, substantial scope tradeoffs, authorship/licensing or submission genuinely needs input. No dates, completion percentages or probability of publication are inferred from the amount of code/documentation.
