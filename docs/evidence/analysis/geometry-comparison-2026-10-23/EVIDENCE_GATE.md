- One historical numerical column is reproduced exactly to its stated rounding: all six BR2005 recalculated-WM means.
- A2 membership alternatives change its reconstructed mean by 1.0696°. Narrative A1 and C cannot be completed using the original numerical table alone.
- For four groups with complete GPS and satellite rows, the final BR2005 directions are outside any convex average of the reconstructed row means under either tested Earth model. This excludes that reduction family, not every possible reduction or the underlying unrounded data.
- Circular versus arithmetic means differ by less than 0.0002° in complete groups. An origin-constrained perpendicular-distance fit differs by up to 0.261°. These are different estimands.
- Absolute-direction rounding envelopes span degrees under the specified permissive nearest-rounding assumptions. They are finite-grid scenarios, not confidence intervals or established survey errors.
- Shared-origin contrasts preserve the northern ordering A3 < A2 < A1 in all tested source-table grids. The smallest sampled A2–A3 separation is 0.617°; A1–A2 is 1.024°. These claims concern the specified grids, not certified continuous bounds, alternative membership or original prehistoric positions.
- The 2025 account documents physical displacement and fragment-centre issues. Therefore contemporary positional accuracy cannot be substituted for reconstruction accuracy.
- Sixteen contextual quarry charcoal determinations are now traceable by laboratory ID. Their shared hearths, stratigraphic positions and occupation/extraction distinctions preclude treating them as sixteen independent installation dates.

## What the pilot does not establish

It does not identify a stellar target or construction date, demonstrate intentionality, invalidate the original survey, derive a probabilistic error distribution, establish a complete ancient monument inventory or demonstrate methodological novelty. It does not show that marginal overlap makes rows indistinguishable: correlated contrasts must be tested directly. It also does not prove that every potential geometric alternative is archaeologically admissible.

## Publication contribution test

The prospective contribution is the explicit separation of reproducibility, reconstructability and astronomical discriminability, demonstrated on a source-critical case. Existing coordinate reassessments and probabilistic orientation methods are comparators, not missing predecessors. A publishable claim needs evidence that this separation changes an archaeological inference or a data-collection decision. The stable relative ordering versus weaker absolute directions is a promising example, but novelty remains to be checked against the full relevant literature.
