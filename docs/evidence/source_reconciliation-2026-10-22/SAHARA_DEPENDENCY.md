# What is lost without Sahara 5:7–16?

23 October 2026. Targeted audit of held sources, not an exhaustive citation search. The missing article itself has not been inspected; its unique contents cannot be enumerated or assigned a recovery percentage.

## Quantified position

- One publication missing: Wendorf, Close and Schild (1992–1993), ten printed pages inclusive, pp.7–16.
- Zero indispensable numerical inputs have so far been identified as available only in this publication. This is a finding about the checked citation trail, not proof the article contains no unique data.
- All six later tabulated alignment groups remain represented in our held coordinate sources. The 100 observation records are four documentary representations of 25 entries (including origin), with two missing GPS coordinate pairs; they are not 100 independent features. Those two missing pairs occur in BR2005's own table and are not demonstrated consequences of the missing Sahara article.
- Eleven chronology records remain available, representing ten labelled determinations plus an alternate reporting of the same DRI-3358 result. Independence of figure labels 2 and 2/BIS is unestablished. None is a direct individual-row construction date. The missing article has not been shown to supply such dates.
- Three checked later accounts explicitly convey the same early geometric interpretation: Nature1998 p.489; BR2005 p.16; Malville2015 p.1085. This is three reports of one historical claim, not three independent surveys.

## Four unverified dimensions of the early report

1. Exact observation set and feature identities: which stones were included or excluded, their early labels, and correspondence to later subdivisions.
2. Original figures and spatial detail: maps, scales, individual measurements or photographs, if supplied. Their number and content are unknown until acquired.
3. Measurement procedure and uncertainty: what justified the roughly 10-degree description, reference north, origin, accuracy and whether it was a measured axis or rough grouping.
4. Original context and qualifications: preservation, archaeological associations, chronology, alternative explanations and how tentative the interpretation was.

These are four audit dimensions, not a claim that four datasets exist in the missing publication. Later sources cover parts of them, but do not allow complete verification of the early account.

## Effect on the proposed paper

Core later-source geometry and source-conflict analysis: can proceed. No present central numerical result requires guessing a Sahara value.

Comprehensive discovery-history reconstruction: incomplete. Cite the early 10-degree interpretation as reported by Nature1998, rather than implying direct reading of Sahara.

Resolving A1/A2 membership, A-10, C-6/C-8, original standing positions, and quarry-to-row chronology: already unresolved in the held publications. The early paper might help with some identities but is not established as the remedy. The quarry measurements reported in later sources should not be assumed to occur in the early report.

Publication consequence: disclose the inaccessible source and bound historical claims. Seek the article in parallel; do not make acquisition a blanket gate for continuing analysis. A claim uniquely depending on it must remain unresolved or be omitted if the paper cannot be obtained.
