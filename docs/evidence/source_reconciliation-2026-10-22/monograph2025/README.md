## What changes

1. **Archaeological position is distinct from measurement precision.** Chapter7 describes erosion/creep and fallen fragments across the A and B alignments. The concern is not confined to excluded C. More accurate modern positions alone cannot recover original placement.
2. **A-10 is not absent from the archaeological record.** Page140 identifies its embedded base and Sector B square j’/17; the earlier numeric table lacks its coordinate. Map/drawing recovery is now a concrete route, but a grid square is not a surveyed point. A-W/A-Y/A-Z broaden the documented physical inventory without automatically becoming members of a historical row.
3. **Survey documentation exists beyond the older rounded tables.** Pages23–24 describe later total-station mapping and WGS84/UTM36N conversion. The explicit 2009–2011 six-thousand-point survey concerns Gebel El Muqaddas; its precision must not be transferred uncritically to every radial-row position. The compass orientation and mapping transformations need inspection before adopting map north as true north.
4. **Quarry dates have identifiable contexts.** Seven E-04-4 charcoal determinations are transcribed separately. Several share the same hearth; they are not automatically independent construction episodes. The 2015 figure's five matching values suggest lineage, but numeric equality alone does not establish its sample-label crosswalk.
5. **Chronology interpretation is revised.** Pages104–105 favour quarrying/installation broadly around 4600–4200/4100 BC, while explicitly leaving undated pits and possible later surface extraction. They treat the younger DRI-3358 charcoal (4800±85 BP) as potentially unrelated to stela installation. The ±85 value is supported by this later text, but does not erase the earlier ±80 transcription. Individual stelae still lack direct construction dates.
6. **Later stellar-fit dates face a documented archaeological challenge.** Page139 contrasts an older and younger proposed phase and judges the latter too recent relative to its revised chronology. This is an author interpretation to evaluate with the contexts, not a licence to exclude dates solely because they disagree with a preferred astronomical target.

## Remaining work and interpretation boundaries

- Reconcile all sixteen quarry measurements and their stratigraphic roles, including older/reworked material, before any pooled chronological model.
- Check map grid dimensions, north convention, origin identification and scan/digitization precision before deriving missing coordinates. Preserve 2025 grid labels separately from earlier survey labels.
- Do not treat a modern fragment centre as an ancient observation target without a physical reconstruction argument.
- Keep existing numerical findings as analyses of the older published tables. Claims that the current archaeological record lacks maps, laboratory IDs or any position for A-10 are now superseded.
- The core question should distinguish: what can be reproduced from a publication; what physical alignment can be reconstructed; and what astronomical interpretation can be discriminated. Those are three different evidence requirements.

## Extended quarry inspection

Read the remaining chapter5 material through pp.89–103; all sixteen listed charcoal determinations are now in quarry_all16.csv. Page101 values were checked visually. The site groups are seven E-04-4, one E-06-2, two E-05-2 and six E-05-3. Individual samples are not sixteen independent construction events.

Page102 explicitly distinguishes use of the quarry depressions for occupation from extraction in a particular part of the quarry. Dating such fill/hearths does not directly date the extraction of a particular slab. Do not pool the sixteen measurements into one construction date. Page104's retrospective context labels also differ from the detailed listings: Poz-73336 is assigned to Hearth2 at p.101 but called Bed6a and later Hearth3 in the summary; Poz-19188's detailed listing is Hearth2 near Unit4 base at p.93, versus Bed3 base in the summary. Preserve these discrepancies and use the detailed context as a documented scenario, not an unacknowledged correction.

The two older results (5980±110 and 6020±40 BP) must not be discarded simply for lying outside the preferred younger grouping. Their archaeological context and possible earlier quarrying/occupation are substantive alternatives.
