## Combining choices

Use three tiers: (1) source-supported documentary alternatives; (2) explicit computational assumptions needed to reproduce or bound them; (3) counterfactual stress tests. Keep tiers visible in every result. A Tier 3 failure does not refute Tier 1 evidence; a Tier 2 success does not establish original positions.

Start with source-matched table memberships and the shared source origin. Combine recording conventions with those matched datasets. Test original-WM narrative alternatives only where coordinates are complete. Separate an origin-displacement experiment from the basic coordinate-recording test; combine them only in a separately labelled experiment without double-counting the same origin uncertainty. Preserve one variable per coordinate when a point is shared.

The eligible comparison inventory includes every pair of the six table-defined groups, with completeness and scientific relevance recorded first. The three northern pairwise contrasts and B1/B2 are the within-sector comparisons; cross-sector comparisons are documentary controls, not equally meaningful archaeological hypotheses. Publish exclusions rather than treating the available subset as a complete ancient monument population.

## Claim provenance

- Reproduction of historical means is a benchmark against a published column; it validates a reduction of printed inputs only.
- Membership/estimator comparisons and the northern-ordering result emerged during exploration. Record them as such; no retrospective preregistration language.
- The A-X/A-4 conflict emerged from later-source reconciliation and remains conditional on identity and map interpretation.
- Context intersections are calculations on published interpretations, not independently dated row construction.
