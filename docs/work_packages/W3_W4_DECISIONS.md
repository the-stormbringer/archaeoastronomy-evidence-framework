# W3 Method extensions and W4 contribution decision

These decisions remain pending. They belong to the main agent and ultimately to the research authors, not to delegate workers.

## W3 Extension decisions

For each proposed addition, record: question; plausible change to the conclusion; data required; existing method to compare against; assumptions introduced; cost/complexity; decisive result; and stop condition.

E5 synthetic validation proceeds only after a locked design specifies geometry, observation formation, corruptions, metrics, seeds or deterministic cases and equal-information comparators. Development simulations cannot demonstrate that a disturbance occurred at Nabta. E6 astronomy proceeds only if the physical quantity, fixed target hypothesis, horizon/visibility conventions and chronology support a noncircular question. Otherwise retain the documentary scope.

A reasoned decision not to add an experiment can close that branch. It must not be disguised as a completed experiment.

## W4 Contribution decision template

- Proposed contribution in one sentence.
- Direct predecessor and the precise difference from it.
- Strongest supported result and its archaeological consequence.
- Strongest alternative explanation and the evidence that addresses it.
- Assumptions under which the result changes or fails.
- Negative/inconclusive outcomes that constrain the claim.
- What independent reviewer or new evidence could overturn the conclusion.
- Chosen paper form and reasons.

Permitted outcomes: strengthened full case study; narrower source-critical or replication note; a defined further-evidence route; or no submission of the present claim set. The number of scripts, sources or pages is not a criterion for choosing a full paper.

After this decision, W5 revises the manuscript and package around accepted claims. W6 requires external specialist scrutiny, external reproduction and author/venue/licence decisions. W7 designs a genuinely independent follow-up. No automatic publication, correspondence or remote push is authorized by these skeletons.
